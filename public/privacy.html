Privacy policy

Last updated: November 10, 2026

LufiRoom is an ambient sound app: you tap the objects in a 3D room to start music, rain, wind and more. Here is, plainly, what it does with your data: almost nothing.

In short

  • No account, no sign-up.
  • No personal data collected.
  • No ads, no tracking.
  • Your preferences stay on your device.

What is stored on your device

To bring your room back the way you left it, LufiRoom keeps the following in your device's local storage (localStorage): language, selected room, weather, master volume, each room's radio station, your saved mixes and your last mix. This information never leaves your device and we have no access to it. You can erase it at any time by clearing the site's data in your browser or by uninstalling the app.

Sharing a mix

The share link encodes the mix (the sounds and their volumes) in the address itself. No data is sent to a server to create it. What happens next (sending it to someone) depends on the messaging app you choose.

Website hosting

The website is hosted by Cloudflare. Like any host, Cloudflare may keep standard technical logs (IP address, date, requested page) to run and secure the service. We do not use them to track you. Once installed, the mobile app works offline with its bundled sounds.

Audience measurement

On the website only, cookie-free audience measurement that does not follow you across sites may be enabled to know how many people visit. It is not part of the mobile app.

Fonts and third parties

Fonts are bundled with the app: nothing is sent to Google or any other font service. LufiRoom includes no advertising, no tracking tool and no social network widget.

Children

LufiRoom is not specifically aimed at children and collects data about no one, children included.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top of the page will be updated.
